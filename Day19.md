# Agentic AI Fundamentals - Agent or Not an Agent?

## 1. Email Auto-Reply Rule
**Not an Agent**

Justification: It follows predefined rules and responds automatically without reasoning or decision-making.

## 2. FAQ Chatbot
**Not an Agent**

Justification: It answers user questions but cannot independently plan or perform actions to achieve goals.

## 3. AI Meeting Scheduler
**Agent**

Justification: It checks calendars, selects available time slots, and sends invitations to complete a task autonomously.

## 4. File Backup Automation
**Not an Agent**

Justification: It executes a fixed workflow and cannot adapt its behavior based on changing objectives.

## 5. Customer Support AI Assistant
**Agent**

Justification: It can analyze issues, use tools, retrieve information, and take actions to resolve customer requests.

## Conclusion

- Automation follows predefined rules.
- Chatbots primarily provide conversational responses.
- Agents can reason, plan, use tools, and perform actions to achieve goals.
